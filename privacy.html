FRIDAY Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

FRIDAY is a private assistant used only by its owner, Charles Nicholson. The only Google account it signs in to is his own.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, FRIDAY reads his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and YouTube data through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show, summarize and organize the owner's own information for him. It is processed on his own computers.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising. FRIDAY's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's machine. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
